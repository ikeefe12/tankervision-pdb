# First-board control test — 2026-09-14

**Result: converter/port sequence passed 53 checks with zero failures.** Charger
CE assertion/deassertion and charger input PG also passed in the open-TS test.
This establishes the tested control and PG paths, not complete board qualification.

Hardware: ESP32-S3 revision v0.2, 8 MB flash, 8 MB embedded PSRAM, MAC
`68:ee:8f:58:b0:10`, USB Serial/JTAG at `/dev/cu.usbmodem2101`.
Main capture began at **19:00:34 UTC / 15:00:34 America/Toronto**.

| Test | Observed result |

| Input baseline | `PG_USB=1`, `PG_DC=0`, `5V_SS_PG=1`, `PMUX_ST=1` |

Main evidence is [final-hardware.log](final-hardware.log). The earlier
[basic-first.log](basic-first.log) recorded 46 passes before continuous dwell
checks were added. The later upload changes only the charger's result wording to
say actual current was unmeasured; it does not change control/test behavior.
See [upload.log](upload.log), [build.log](build.log), and
[host-tests.log](host-tests.log). The original 8 MB flash was saved locally in
`pre-test-flash.bin`, ignored by Git.

[Final boot/status](final-status.log) at 19:03:47 UTC confirms initialization
passed after the final upload, all controlled outputs are off, and VCAP has
returned to 0.000 V. [build-manifest.json](build-manifest.json) records source and
uploaded binary SHA-256 hashes.

The hardware log's original phrase “TS inhibits power conversion” expresses the
expected protection behavior, **not a verified current measurement**. The final
firmware wording makes that limitation explicit. With CE low before the tests,
VCAP read 0.000 V. During the open-TS CE test it reached approximately **0.616 V**;
immediately after shutdown it read approximately **0.478 V**. TS remained about
**2.908–2.911 V**. The cause of that small VCAP reading is undetermined; verify it
with a DMM/scope before concluding anything about leakage, switching, or ADC error.

The ADC snapshot returned TS 2909 mV, VCAP ADC 53 mV, and all seven IMON channels
0 mV. These are uncalibrated bench observations. IMON is unspecified below 0.5 A;
the USB IMON reading does not mean the operating board consumed zero current.

Eight host tests compile the **actual BoardControl.cpp** with mocked I²C/GPIO and
`-Wall -Wextra -Werror`. All pass: missing internal/external expander recovery,
stuck CE protection, retained polarity, every input mapping, J8 dependency and
shutdown guard, missing buck PG rollback, and CE-before-boost shutdown timing.
These test software failure handling, not injected faults on the physical board.

Still unverified: actual charge current and regulation, high-current selection,
thermistor fault response at the charger pin, backup switch PG with a charged bank,
source transfer, connector voltages/current capacity, ADC calibration, PD negotiated
budget, expander/PD interrupts, Jetson interface, and deliberate retained-on reset
recovery. The connected loads, bank, thermistor, and source rating were not reported.
The remaining setup and procedures are in the [test matrix](../README.md).

The backed buck and LDOs are hardware-enabled; firmware cannot switch them
individually. Corrected documentation now matches the schematic PG input order
and the actual D12 PG / D13 STAT LED wiring. No schematic or PCB electrical edits
were made during this firmware test.
